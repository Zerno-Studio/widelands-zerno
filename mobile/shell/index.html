Touch guide

Tap to select. Swipe with one finger on the map to move it. Drag a window or slider to adjust it. Hold to inspect without clicking.

Move two fingers together to move the map. Spread to zoom in; pinch to zoom out. A two-finger gesture never builds anything.

Use your device’s Back button to close a window or open the game menu. Close window below is the alternative to a secondary click.

Commands lists the game’s own shortcuts. Some commands work only in their relevant window; unavailable bindings are shown disabled.

Extra options for the next tap

Ctrl: multiple selection or road flags. Shift: ranges or alternate values. These also combine. The selected options clear after one tap.
